What we do

Two licensed disciplines, one team, in your living room.

Most families need more than one kind of help. Rather than coordinating two practices, you get physical therapy and occupational therapy from the same office, working off the same plan.

Getting you moving again — safely, and in the place you'll be doing the moving.

Post-surgical recovery

Hip and knee replacements, spine surgery, rotator cuff, fracture repair. We pick up where the hospital left off.

Balance & fall prevention

Steadier walking, better reaction time, and the confidence to move around your own house without hovering near a wall.

Hands-on manual therapy

Joint mobilization and soft-tissue work for tight, painful muscles — the part you can't get from a printed exercise sheet.

Strength & endurance

Progressive strengthening built around what you're trying to get back to, not a generic protocol.

Walking & gait training

Cane, walker, or nothing at all — we work on the surfaces you cross every day, including your own carpet and thresholds.

Neurological recovery

Stroke, Parkinson's, MS and neuropathy — retraining movement and neuromuscular control in daily settings.

    Questions

    The things people ask on the first call

    Do I need a referral from my doctor?

    It depends on your insurance plan and your situation. Call us and we'll check it for you in a few minutes — and if a referral is needed, we'll tell you exactly what to ask your physician for.

    What does the therapist bring?

    Everything needed for a session — hands-on treatment tools, resistance equipment, and assessment gear. You don't need to buy or set up anything beforehand.

    How long is a visit, and how often?

    Visits generally run about an hour, one-on-one. Frequency depends on your diagnosis and plan of care — that gets set at the evaluation, and adjusted as you improve.

    Is home therapy as effective as going to a clinic?

    For a lot of goals it has a real advantage: the therapist can see the actual stairs, the actual bathroom, and the actual furniture you're struggling with, and train you on them directly. Some conditions do call for specialized clinic equipment — if that's your situation, we'll say so on the first call rather than after you've signed up.

    My house is small and cluttered. Is that a problem?

    Not at all, and please don't clean up for us. Seeing the house as it normally is tells us more about your risk of falling than a tidied version would.

    Do you treat both physical and occupational therapy needs?

    Yes — both, from the same practice. If you need PT and OT, you're not coordinating two separate companies or repeating your history twice.

    Let's get you moving around your own house again.

    Tell us what's going on and we'll check your coverage. No obligation, and no forms until you've spoken to a person.
