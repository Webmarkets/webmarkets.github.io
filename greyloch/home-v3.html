Why Builders Choose Greyloch

At Greyloch, we don't do one-size-fits-all. Every cabinet system we build is designed and manufactured specifically for the project at hand — matching your layout, your timeline, and your client's vision down to the last detail.
From new home builds and kitchen renovations to multi-family developments and commercial interiors, our cabinetry is trusted by builders and contractors across the region to deliver quality that's consistent, beautiful, and built to last.

What we build: Kitchens and bathrooms for custom homes and renovations, bathroom vanities and storage, built-in office and workspace cabinetry, and apartment and multi-family housing developments. Our frameless, European-style cabinetry brings clean lines and smart storage to any space — equally at home in a sleek modern kitchen or a high-traffic commercial interior. With both painted and stained finishes available alongside our premium panel product line, your clients get a look that's truly their own. Whatever you're building, we're ready to build it with you.

Where Technology Meets Craftsmanship

Precision Cabinet Manufacturing in Star, Idaho

Greyloch operates a large manufacturing facility in Star designed to produce high-quality cabinetry with consistency and efficiency. By combining advanced automated equipment with experienced craftsmanship, our team maintains strict quality standards while supporting projects of all sizes.

Our manufacturing process focuses on:

  • Precision cutting and edgebanding
  • Consistent cabinet construction and assembly
  • Efficient production timelines
  • Durable materials designed for long-term use

This approach allows Greyloch to support both individual homeowners and large-scale development projects with dependable cabinetry solutions.
