How It's Made

Material first. Machine-precise. Human-finished.

No. 01 — The Material

Grain-matched, hand-selected

Rift white oak, walnut, and painted maple are selected and sequenced by hand so drawer fronts and door panels read as one continuous surface, the details you feel before you notice them.

No. 02 — The Machine

Cut to a tenth of a millimeter

German Homag machinery cuts, edge-bands, drills, and fits hardware with near-zero variance. Automation isn't a compromise on craft, it's what protects it at volume.

No. 03 — The Hand

Finished and installed by ours

Every piece is inspected, finished, and installed by Greyloch's own team, from our Star, Idaho workshop to your job site, phased exactly to your build schedule.
