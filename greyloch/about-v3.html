Where Quality Meets Modern Design

Built on Innovation & Craftsmanship

Shaun Fickes' journey from learning woodworking in junior and senior high school to founding Greyloch LLC is a testament to the evolution of passion into profession. Initially a hobby, Shaun's woodworking skills gradually transitioned into a full-fledged career alongside his wife, Dianne. Their entrepreneurial journey began in 1991 with Custom Oak Furnishings, operating out of Shaun's father's garage. As demand grew, Shaun seized an opportunity to expand into kitchen cabinet making and changed the business name to Greyloch Custom Cabinetry. With steadfast determination, Shaun, aided by his father, a small team, and loyal customers, tirelessly grew the business. Long hours and nurturing relationships with builders and suppliers became the norm as Shaun refused to settle for mediocrity.

However, it was a transformative trip to Europe in 2015 that ignited Shaun's vision for revolutionizing cabinet manufacturing in America. Inspired by European practices, Shaun embarked on a mission to introduce cutting-edge automation to Greyloch. Collaborating with industry partners, this vision materialized into a state-of-the-art, fully automated factory. By 2021, the company rebranded as Greyloch LLC, marking a new era of innovation and growth. In the fall of 2023, production commenced in a sprawling 97,000 sq. ft. facility in Star, Idaho, the culmination of years of dedication and hard work.

Throughout this remarkable journey, Shaun and Dianne express immense gratitude to their support network - family, friends, customers, vendors, and above all, their dedicated employees. Their unwavering support and commitment have been instrumental in shaping Greyloch into the thriving enterprise it is today.

Where Quality Meets Modern Design

Built on Innovation & Craftsmanship

Greyloch was founded with a commitment to creating custom cabinets that balance quality craftsmanship with modern design. What began as a small cabinet operation in Meridian in the early 1990s has grown into one of the region's most advanced cabinet manufacturing facilities. Over the years, the company has continued to invest in technology and manufacturing processes to produce cabinetry with greater accuracy and efficiency.

This focus on innovation helps Greyloch support a wide range of clients, including:

  • Homeowners planning kitchen renovations or new builds
  • Contractors and builders managing residential construction
  • Designers developing modern interior spaces
  • Developers overseeing multi-unit housing and commercial projects

Every cabinet produced by Greyloch is designed to meet the specific needs of the project while maintaining the clean, modern look that has become the brand's signature.

Precision Manufacturing at Scale

A State-of-the-Art Manufacturing Facility

Greyloch operates a 97,000-square-foot cabinet manufacturing facility in Star, Idaho, designed to support efficient production without sacrificing quality.

The facility is equipped with advanced automation technology developed by HOMAG in Germany, allowing the team to transform raw materials into finished cabinetry through a streamlined production process.

This system includes automated cutting, edgebanding, drilling, and assembly processes that maintain consistent cabinet construction while maximizing material efficiency.

Key capabilities of the facility include:

01

Material Optimization

Optimized material usage to reduce waste.

02

Automated Cutting

Automated material cutting and panel processing.

03

Precision Edgebanding

Precision edgebanding and drilling technology.

04

Integrated Production

Integrated systems that manage thousands of cabinet parts during production.

This technology allows Greyloch to maintain dependable timelines while producing cabinetry that meets strict quality standards.

Clean Lines, Contemporary Finishes

Modern Cabinetry Designed for Today’s Spaces

Greyloch specializes in European frameless cabinetry, known for its clean lines, modern design, and efficient storage layout.

Their cabinet systems are available in a variety of finishes that complement contemporary interiors, including wood-grain textures, matte finishes, and high-gloss panels.

These cabinet designs are commonly used in:

  • Residential kitchens and bathrooms
  • Office environments
  • Apartment and multi-family housing
  • Retail and commercial spaces
  • Custom storage and furniture applications

Each cabinet is built using premium materials and inspected throughout the production process to ensure long-term durability and consistent performance.
