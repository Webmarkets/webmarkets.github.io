01 — Color

A brighter green,
without the glare.

Circuit Green keeps the energy of the old fluorescent green but sits comfortably on the eye — closer to a well-lit space than a highlighter. It reads as "on" against Blackout, and stays legible as a text color in its deep form.

PRIMARY
Circuit Green#35C96B

The brand accent. Buttons, glows, live indicators, key numbers.

TEXT-SAFE
Deep Circuit#1FA954

Hover states and green text on light backgrounds — passes contrast where Circuit Green can't.

WARM
2700K Amber#F6A93B

The warm counterpoint, named for lamp color temperature. Tiny doses: kicker slashes, highlights.

LIGHTS OFF
Blackout#0F1519

Dark sections — the "before" every retrofit starts from. Where green glows brightest.

SURFACE
Panel#161D23

Raised cards and surfaces on Blackout sections.

DEFAULT
Daylight#FAFBF8

Page background — a warm off-white, never sterile gray. Band (#F0F3EE) is its softer sibling.

75%

Daylight and Band. The page should feel like a well-lit room — open and calm.

20%

Blackout and ink. One or two dark bands per page where the work shows off.

5%

Circuit Green + a pinch of Amber. If everything glows, nothing does.
