Financing Alternatives

Pay for it without
CAPEX dollars.

Reluctant to fund a lighting upgrade with capital dollars? We offer alternatives that can pay for 100% of the upgrade — and you keep any available utility rebate.

FIN-01 · PACE

Property Assessed Clean Energy

We specialize in PACE funding — long-term financing tied to the property, not the balance sheet, covering up to 100% of your retrofit cost.

Ask About PACE
FIN-02 · LAAS

Lumens-As-A-Service

We'll buy, install, own, and maintain your lighting system — you simply pay for the light with OPEX dollars, like any other utility.
