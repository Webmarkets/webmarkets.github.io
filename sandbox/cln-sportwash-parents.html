High School Contact Sports Carry a Skin Infection Risk Most Parents Don't Know About

Every wrestling mat, every shared piece of football equipment, every locker room bench your athlete uses is a potential source of skin infection. Staph bacteria. Ringworm. Impetigo. MRSA. These aren't rare. Athletic trainers and team physicians deal with them regularly. They sideline athletes for days or weeks at a time. And most standard soap and body wash offer no meaningful protection against them.

This Isn't a Hygiene Problem. It's a Medical One.

Physicians who work with contact sport athletes know that shared training environments create a fundamentally different kind of exposure than everyday life. The bacteria and fungi that cause skin infections in sports settings thrive in warm, humid, high-contact conditions.

Regular body wash is designed to remove visible dirt. It isn't formulated to address the pathogens responsible for the infections that pull athletes off the field.

There is a difference. And most families don't realize it until an infection has already happened.
