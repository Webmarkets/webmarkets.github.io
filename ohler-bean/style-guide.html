Brand Style Guide

A reference for the visual language of Ohler Bean — the colors, typography, and components that convey our standard of counsel: composed, precise, and unmistakably premium.

We represent our clients with the discretion, rigor, and clarity their matters demand — pairing seasoned judgment with a relentless attention to detail at every stage of counsel.

Typography

Display — Ariana Pro

Counsel, Refined.

Usage: headlines, section titles, and callout copy. Use Regular or Medium weight.

Light Regular Medium Semibold

Body — Inter

Inter carries all body copy and long-form content. With generous line-height and a restrained weight, it keeps complex legal material approachable and easy to read across every screen.

Usage: paragraphs, descriptions, UI text, and fine print.

Eyebrow / Label — Inter, letterspaced

Practice Areas · 01

Usage: overlines, labels, and small navigational markers. Uppercase, 600 weight, wide tracking.

04

Corner Radius

Restrained Radius

6px · 0.375rem

A tight, uniform 6px radius is applied across cards, buttons, inputs, and media. The subtle softening keeps the interface modern and approachable while preserving the crisp, structured feel a legal brand requires.

05

Spacing & Layout

8-Point Scale

Disciplined Whitespace

Generous, consistent spacing built on an 8px base gives the brand its unhurried, high-end composure. Use these steps for padding, gaps, and margins.

11

Imagery Style

Rich & Tonal

Depth: Lean on deep, saturated tones and soft, directional light for a premium, understated mood.

Tactile Materials

Texture: Feature natural materials — leather, velvet, brass, and wood — to signal craft and permanence.

Brand Wash

Tint: Apply subtle Deep Sea or Forest overlays to photography for a consistent, cohesive palette.

12

Motion

Gentle Lift
Slow Fade

Motion is slow, subtle, and purposeful. Favor soft fades and small lifts (200–400ms, ease-in-out). Avoid bounces or fast, attention-seeking animation — restraint signals confidence.

13

Elevation

Level 1
Level 2
Level 3

Shadows are soft and low-contrast, tinted with Deep Sea rather than pure black. Use Level 1 for resting cards, Level 2 on hover, and Level 3 for overlays and modals.
