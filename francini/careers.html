Employee Benefits

Taken Care Of, From Day One

01
Paid Time Off

Our employees are offered flexible Paid Time Off (PTO) for vacation time plus sick leave. Vacation time accrues at a higher rate as your duration with the company increases.

02
Medical Benefits

Employees are eligible for coverage following 30 calendar days. We pay 75% of employees' medical premiums and offer options for dental and employees' families.

03
Life Insurance

We pay 100% of the employee life insurance premium.

04
Retirement

After one year of employment, employees are eligible to enroll in our 401K Retirement Plan.
