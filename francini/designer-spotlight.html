3 Ivy Design's Primary Bath Transformation with Francini Stone

The Project · Bathroom Transformation

A well-designed bathroom should be both a retreat and a functional space

A well-designed bathroom should be both a retreat and a functional space, seamlessly blending beauty and practicality. That’s exactly what 3 Ivy Design achieved in their latest primary bath project, transforming a simple space into a sophisticated sanctuary. By selecting Francini’s Calacatta Gold Premium large-format porcelain from the Houston Slab Gallery, the design team crafted a modern yet timeless aesthetic that elevates everyday luxury.

From a stunning statement shower to seamless countertops and a porcelain-clad ceiling, every detail in this project reflects the designers’ meticulous attention to elegance and cohesion. Here’s how 3 Ivy Design brought this vision to life with Francini’s natural stone.

3 Ivy Design

How 3 Ivy Design Blended Modern and Timeless Elegance in This Primary Bath

For this project, 3 Ivy Design was inspired by their clients’ desire for a bathroom that felt contemporary yet enduring—a space that would look just as beautiful years from now as it does today. Their solution? A statement shower with sleek, large-format porcelain that extends seamlessly from the walls to the ceiling and countertops.

The use of mitered edges throughout the design enhances the fluidity of the space, eliminating sharp transitions and creating a refined, cohesive look. The result is a bathroom that feels expansive, polished, and effortlessly sophisticated—a perfect example of 3 Ivy Design’s commitment to crafting spaces that are both approachable and elevated.

The Beauty and Benefits of Francini’s Calacatta Gold Large-Format Porcelain

To achieve the perfect balance of durability and luxury, Francini’s Calacatta Gold Premium large-format porcelain was the material of choice for this primary bath. Unlike natural marble, porcelain is highly durable, non-porous, and resistant to stains and moisture, making it an ideal option for bathroom applications.

The benefits of using Francini’s premium porcelain in this project include:

  • Luxurious appearance with the timeless beauty of Calacatta marble
  • Durability that withstands moisture, heat, and daily use
  • Low maintenance and easy-to-clean surfaces
  • Seamless integration with minimal grout lines for a sleek, modern finish

By specifically choosing large-format slabs, 3 Ivy Design was able to minimize grout lines, creating a smooth, uninterrupted flow that enhances the room’s modern aesthetic.

Whether used for shower walls, countertops, or backsplashes, Francini’s Calacatta Gold porcelain brings effortless sophistication to any space. This exquisite stone mimics the look of classic Calacatta marble, with delicate veining and a luminous white base that exudes elegance.
