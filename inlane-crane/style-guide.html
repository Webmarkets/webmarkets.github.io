Brand & UI Style Guide

Inland Crane

A professional rebuild on the colors Inland Crane already owns — the logo's deep navy and crane gold — with industrial signage type and the structure of the machines themselves: lattice bracing, rigging stripes, and boom-angle cuts.

01

Logo

The current mark stays — navy oval, banner, and the gold hook block. On dark backgrounds it rides on a white plate so the oval never disappears into the navy.

Primary — on white & light surfaces, over subtle lattice
On navy — white plate, rigging stripe below

04

Crane structure in the design

The machines shape the system. Lattice-boom cross-bracing becomes a background texture, rigging hardware becomes the gold stripe, and load-chart measurement ticks underline every section heading.

Lattice bracing — section & panel texture
Rigging stripe — gold/navy band on dark edges
Measurement ticks — heading underlines
Boom-angle panels

Dark bands get a boom cut — the bottom edge slices at the working angle of a luffing boom, so even the section breaks carry the silhouette of the fleet.

05

Buttons & CTAs

Gold is the action color — it's the hook that does the lifting. The phone number rides shotgun on every page, Consurco-style.
