Know the signs.

Understanding the symptoms and effects of chronic pain can help you manage it better. Getting treatment is important to reduce symptoms and improve your quality of life — our goal at Apex is to address which options are best to relieve your pain.

Find Relief Today
  • Ongoing pain

    Lasts longer than three months — often a constant, dull (or not so dull) ache.

  • Limited movement

    Pain can make it harder to move around and do everyday tasks.

  • Fatigue

    Trouble sleeping, changes in mood, or a lower appetite often follow.

  • Depression & anxiety

    Constant pain can affect mental health and create feelings of stress or helplessness.

  • Social withdrawal

    Pain makes it harder to stay active socially, leading to isolation or loneliness.

  • Lower quality of life

    Chronic pain interferes with work, relationships, and daily activities.
