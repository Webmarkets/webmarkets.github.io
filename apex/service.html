About the Treatment

Relief, delivered precisely where it hurts.

When a nerve in your spine is compressed or inflamed, pain can radiate into your arms or legs. An epidural steroid injection places medication directly into the epidural space to calm that inflammation — often providing relief that lasts for months.

  • Commonly treats sciatica, herniated discs, and spinal stenosis
  • Performed with X-ray (fluoroscopic) guidance for precision
  • A quick outpatient visit — most patients go home within the hour
Is This You?

Symptoms we treat.

  • Chronic neck stiffness
  • Radiating pain into shoulders or arms
  • Headaches that originate in the neck
  • Numbness or tingling
  • Pain that worsens with movement
What to Expect

Three simple steps.

01

Consultation

We review your history, imaging, and symptoms to confirm an injection is the right next step for your pain.

02

The Procedure

A brief, image-guided injection performed in-office. Most patients describe only mild pressure and are done in minutes.

03

Recovery & Follow-Up

You'll go home the same day. We follow up to track your relief and plan any next steps in your care.
