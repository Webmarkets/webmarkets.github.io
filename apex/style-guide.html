Apex Advanced Spine & Pain · Design System

Big. Bold. Minimal.

One typeface family in heavy weights, two colors from the logo, and a lot of open space. Every Apex page should feel calm, confident, and instantly readable — nothing decorative that doesn't earn its place.

01 — Color

Navy leads.
Gold punctuates.

Both hues come straight from the logo: the deep blue gradient of the "A" and the gold of the vertebrae. Navy does the heavy lifting; gold appears in small, deliberate doses — an underline, a number, a single button.

Apex Navy#132E58

Primary brand color. Headlines on light, buttons, dark sections.

Apex Blue#2E6DB4

Secondary. Kickers, links, icons, focus states, gradient light end.

Vertebrae Gold#F2B32C

Accent only. Underlines, key numbers, the one high-emphasis CTA.

Deep Navy#0B1C39

Darkest tone. Footers, hover states on navy, text on gold.

Mist#F4F7FB

Soft alternate background for banded sections. Never pure gray.

Apex GradientBlue → Navy

Mirrors the logo mark. Reserved for hero panels and CTA banners.

80%

White and mist. Whitespace is the primary design element — let sections breathe.

15%

Navy and blue. Type, buttons, and one dark band per page at most.

5%

Gold. If gold appears more than three times on a screen, remove one.

02 — Typography

Two families.
Heavy on top.

Sora carries every headline in bold weights with tight tracking. Inter handles everything else. Scale jumps are deliberately large — display type should feel oversized next to body copy.

03 — Buttons

Pills. No noise.

Full-radius pills with bold uppercase labels and generous padding. One gold button per page maximum — it marks the single most important action.

Request Appointment Call Now Our Treatments Learn More

On light: navy is the default, gold is the one high-emphasis action, outline is secondary, and the gold-underlined text link handles inline actions.

Request Appointment Our Treatments

On navy or the Apex gradient: gold becomes the primary, ghost white the secondary.

04 — Logo

Room to breathe.

The mark works on white and deep navy. Keep clear space around it equal to the height of the "APEX" wordmark, and never place it on mid-blue, gold, or photography without a solid backing.

On light

Preferred use. White or mist backgrounds only — never on gray or tinted photography.

On dark

Deep Navy (#0B1C39) backgrounds only, where the gold vertebrae carry the contrast.
