Month-to-Month · No Long-Term Contracts

Pricing and Subscription Plans.

At Executive Allies, our services are delivered through a customizable, month-to-month subscription model. No long-term contracts, no hourly billing, and no surprise invoices. You know exactly what you're getting and what you're paying for every single month.

We offer three tiers of service built around the size and stage of your business, plus a la carte options for specific project needs.

Three Levels of Support

Our Subscription Tiers

Level 01

Financial Guidance

Best for: Businesses with $1 to $3 million in revenue that are ready to hand off their day-to-day accounting.

At this level, we take the entire accounting function off your plate so you can focus on running your business. This is the right fit for founders and owners who are currently managing their own books or overseeing data entry themselves and are ready for a more reliable, professional system.

What's included

  • Accounts payable: bill pay and vendor management
  • Accounts receivable: customer invoicing and collections
  • Payroll processing
  • Cash management
  • Month-end close
  • Financial statement preparation
  • Ongoing education so you understand and can use your numbers
Contact Us to Get Started →

Level 02

Controller Services

Best for: Businesses with $2 to $20 million in revenue and approximately 10 to 40 employees that need accounting leadership overseeing their monthly financial close.

At this level, we provide experienced controller-level oversight of your entire accounting function, along with forward-looking cash flow management to keep you planning ahead.

What's included

  • Everything in Financial Guidance
  • Month-end close oversight and review
  • Balance sheet reconciliation
  • Cash flow management and 8 to 12 week rolling forecast
  • Monthly financial statement review and analysis
  • Internal control review and recommendations
  • Budget and projection development
  • Monthly strategy meeting covering prior month financials, cash flow forecast, and planning for the month ahead
Contact Us to Get Started →

Level 03

Fractional CFO

Best for: Businesses generating more than $15 million in revenue, companies in a rapid growth phase, or businesses preparing for a significant financial event such as a capital raise, acquisition, or ownership transition.

At this level, we become part of your leadership team, providing the strategic financial guidance and executive-level insight that drives your business forward.

What's included

  • Everything in Controller Services
  • Strategic financial planning and modeling
  • KPI development and weekly data analysis
  • Attendance at key investor, director, and leadership meetings
  • Investor and lender communication support
  • Mergers and acquisitions assistance
  • Growth planning and financial forecasting
  • Support through significant financial events and transitions

Frequently Asked

Common Questions About Our Subscriptions

Is there a long-term contract?

No. All of our engagements are month-to-month. We earn your business every month, and you have the flexibility to adjust your subscription as your needs change.

Can I upgrade or downgrade my plan?

Yes. Your subscription is built to grow with your business. If you need more support, we scale up. If your situation changes, we adjust accordingly.

What if I only need help with one specific thing?

We offer a la carte services for clients who have a specific project need rather than ongoing support. Reach out and we'll let you know if it's a good fit.

Do you work with businesses outside of Boise?

Yes. While we're based in Eagle, Idaho and primarily serve businesses across the Treasure Valley, we work with clients throughout the United States.
