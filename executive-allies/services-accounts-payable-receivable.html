The Fundamentals

What Are Accounts Payable and Receivable?

Accounts payable refers to the money your business owes to vendors and suppliers. Accounts receivable refers to the money owed to your business by customers. Managing both well is critical to maintaining healthy cash flow and avoiding the kind of surprises that catch business owners off guard.
