Executive-Level Leadership

What Is a Fractional CFO?

A fractional CFO is an experienced Chief Financial Officer who works with your business on a part-time or contract basis. They provide the same strategic financial leadership you'd expect from a full-time CFO, including growth planning, financial modeling, risk management, and executive-level guidance, without the full-time salary and overhead that comes with it.

For growing businesses that need more than a controller but aren't ready to hire a full-time CFO, fractional CFO services fill that gap completely.

The Right Fit

Who This Is For

Fractional CFO services are the right fit for businesses generating $15 million or more in revenue, companies in a rapid growth phase that need strategic financial leadership, and businesses preparing for a significant financial event such as a capital raise, acquisition, or ownership transition. This service is also a strong option for any business where the owner is making major financial decisions without a strategic financial partner in their corner.
