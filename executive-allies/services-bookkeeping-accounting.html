The Foundation

What Is Bookkeeping and Accounting?

Bookkeeping is the ongoing process of recording and organizing your business's financial transactions. Accounting takes that data and turns it into meaningful financial statements and reports you can actually use to run your business. Together, they form the foundation of every good financial decision.

Without clean, current books, you're making decisions without the full picture. Outdated or inaccurate records make it harder to manage cash flow, apply for financing, file taxes, and plan for growth. When your books are in order, everything else gets easier.
