Common questions

How do I know which tier is right for my finance leader? ▾

The simplest way is a 30-minute discovery call. We listen for three things: (1) the company's revenue and stage, (2) whether there is a defined milestone inside the next 12 months (audit, sponsor cycle, financing, transaction), and (3) how mature the finance team beneath the leader is. From that conversation we recommend a tier.

As a general guide: $30M–$50M companies with no transaction event fit Foundations. $40M–$80M companies preparing for a defined milestone fit Accelerator. $60M–$100M companies actively in a financing, sale, or aggressive-growth phase fit Executive Track.

Is this billed monthly or annually? ▾

Both options are available. The default is monthly billing for the 12-month engagement. Annual prepayment is offered at a 5% discount and includes locked-in pricing for the full year. Payment is processed securely through Stripe.

What is the cancellation policy? ▾

The Accelerator is designed as a 12-month structured program — the outcomes depend on the full arc. That said, life happens. We offer a 30-day notice cancellation policy after Month 2. The first two months are non-refundable to protect the intensive intake and diagnostic investment we make in every participant.

If a participant cancels mid-program, they retain access to the portal and templates library for 30 days post-cancellation.

Can I upgrade or downgrade tiers during the engagement? ▾

Yes. Tier changes are common — often a participant upgrades from Foundations to Accelerator after Month 3 once a new transaction milestone appears, or downgrades from Executive Track to Accelerator after a deal closes. Upgrades take effect immediately. Downgrades take effect at the start of the next quarter. Pricing is pro-rated.

Who are the senior CFO mentors? ▾

Our mentors have served as CFOs, controllers, and senior finance executives at companies with revenues up to $500M+, operating across private-equity-backed, publicly traded, venture-backed, family-owned, and founder-led environments. Collectively, they have led or supported transactions from venture and seed financings through acquisition integrations to multi-billion-dollar divestitures. Several bring Big 4 audit pedigree.

Industry coverage spans SaaS, software, telecommunications, manufacturing, construction, healthcare, professional services, and retail. Mentor assignment is based on best industry and stage fit for your business.

What does the participant get access to immediately after signing up? ▾

Within minutes of payment: full access to the member portal, the templates library, and the curriculum library. Within five business days: a kickoff 1:1 with the assigned mentor and a signed 12-Month Development Plan. The curriculum itself unlocks month by month, so the participant is never overwhelmed but always has a clear next step.

Is enterprise or multi-participant pricing available? ▾

Yes. Companies enrolling two or more finance team members in Foundations or Accelerator receive volume pricing. Multi-entity organizations and companies above $100M revenue with custom requirements are quoted individually. Reach out for a tailored proposal.

Not sure which tier is right?

The shortest path is a 30-minute conversation. We will walk through your finance leader's situation and recommend the tier that fits — or tell you honestly if the program is not the right fit.
