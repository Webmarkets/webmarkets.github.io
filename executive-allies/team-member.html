Meet the Team · Executive Allies

Nate Schrandt.

Co-Founder, CFO | Executive Allies

Contact:

nschrandt@theexecutiveallies.com

Nate learned early that the most important thing you can do when helping a business is listen first. Growing up with a father who was a successful business owner taught him to think outside the box, and those two instincts have guided his entire career.

Nate began at KPMG, where he spent seven years on audit engagements across forest products, manufacturing, internet retail, technology, and healthcare. After leaving public accounting, he served as Controller for several family-owned companies and a large publicly traded healthcare organization. That combination of big-firm rigor and hands-on industry experience is what he brings to every client at Executive Allies.

Outside of work, Nate is a husband and dad to two kids and a mischievous labradoodle named Bear. When he's not at his desk, you'll find him skiing, golfing, mountain biking, hiking, or working through the never-ending list of backyard projects.
