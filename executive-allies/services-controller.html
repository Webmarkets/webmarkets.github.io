Accounting Leadership

What Is a Controller?

A controller is the accounting leader responsible for overseeing your company's financial operations, managing the month-end close process, producing accurate financial statements, and maintaining the internal controls that protect your business. For many growing businesses, a full-time controller isn't necessary yet, but the work they do absolutely is.

What's Included

Month-End Close Oversight

A thorough, organized close process every month so your financials are accurate, on time, and ready to review.

Financial Statement Preparation and Review

Profit and Loss Statements, Balance Sheets, and Cash Flow Statements prepared and reviewed each month, with the context you need to understand what the numbers actually mean.

Balance Sheet Reconciliation

We reconcile every balance sheet account so you can trust your books and catch any issues before they become bigger problems.

Cash Flow Management and Forecasting

An 8 to 12 week rolling cash flow forecast reviewed at every monthly meeting so you're always planning ahead.

Internal Control Review

We evaluate your accounting processes and identify gaps where risk of error, inefficiency, or fraud may exist, then help you put stronger controls in place.

Budget and Projection Development

We build budgets based on your past performance and forward-looking goals, then track your progress against them throughout the year.

The Right Fit

Who This Is For

Controller services are the right fit for businesses generating roughly $2 million to $20 million in revenue with around 10 to 40 employees that need an accounting leader overseeing their monthly financial close but aren't ready to bring one on full time. This is also a strong option for businesses that have recently experienced accounting department turnover and need experienced interim coverage.
