Visibility Ahead

What Is Cash Flow Forecasting?

A cash flow forecast projects your expected cash inflows and outflows over a defined period, typically 8 to 12 weeks. It shows you not just whether your business is profitable, but whether you'll have the cash on hand to meet your obligations when they come due. A business can be profitable on paper and still run into serious trouble if cash flow isn't actively managed.

82%

According to research from U.S. Bank, 82% of small businesses that fail do so because of cash flow problems. A regularly updated forecast gives you the visibility to spot problems early and act before they become crises.
