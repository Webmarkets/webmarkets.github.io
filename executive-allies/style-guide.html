Typography

Display - Manrope

Accelerate. Deliver. Lead.

Usage: headlines, tier names, page titles, and callout copy. Use Bold (700) or ExtraBold (800).

Light Regular Medium Semibold Bold ExtraBold

Body - Inter

Inter carries all long-form and UI copy. At a comfortable line-height and Regular weight, it keeps structured program information clear, scannable, and approachable for busy executives and finance leaders.

Usage: body paragraphs, feature lists, descriptions, fine print, and UI text.

10

Imagery Style

Professional Settings

Context: Conference rooms, boardrooms, and financial workspaces - clean and authoritative.

People & Mentorship

Tone: Candid, forward-looking. Show engagement and dialogue, not staged poses.

Navy Overlay

Treatment: Apply a Navy color wash (50–70% opacity) to photography for brand consistency.

11

Motion

Card Lift (hover)
Pulse / Emphasis

Motion is fast and purposeful - 150–200ms ease transitions for interactive states (hover, focus). Avoid slow animations; the Executive Allies audience values efficiency and speed. Use subtle lifts (translateY -3–4px) and opacity fades only.

12

Elevation

Level 1
Level 2
Level 3

Shadows are tinted with rgba(15, 34, 64, …) - the Navy Deeper color - rather than pure black. Level 1 for resting cards; Level 2 on hover and active states; Level 3 for the featured tier card and modals.
