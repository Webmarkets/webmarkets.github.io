Our Story

We exist to fill the gap.

Executive Allies is an outsourced accounting and fractional CFO firm based in Eagle, Idaho. We work with small and mid-sized businesses across the Treasure Valley that need real financial expertise without the cost of a full-time hire. Our team of CPAs, controllers, and CFOs steps in as your accounting department, handling everything from day-to-day bookkeeping to strategic financial leadership.

We started Executive Allies because we saw a gap that too many growing businesses fall into: needing more than a bookkeeper but not quite ready for a full-time controller or CFO. We fill that gap completely, and we've been doing it ever since.
