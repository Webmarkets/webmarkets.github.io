Brand & UI Style Guide

CTR — calm after the damage.

Simple, clean, concise. The colors come straight from the CTR logo — navy for water, trust, and calm; red saved for the one moment that matters: the call. A Servpro-strength hero with the warmth and whitespace of Kiwi Restoration.

01

Design principles

The current site has over a hundred links on the homepage. The redesign earns trust by doing less: someone with a flooded kitchen needs the phone number, not a sitemap.

6

Links in the nav. Ever.

Water · Fire · Mold · Commercial · About · Contact. Everything else lives one level deeper.

1

Red button per screen.

Red means "call now" and nothing else. If everything is urgent, nothing is.

1

Idea per section.

Big type, short copy, generous whitespace. Scan the page in ten seconds, find help in one.

02

Logo

The current mark stays — the red CTR badge with the full name beside it. On navy it sits on a white plate so the wordmark never sinks into the background.

On navy — white plate, generous padding

Montserrat for headings — sturdy, modern, friendly — and Open Sans for body copy. The same pairing Kiwi Restoration uses, and already in CTR's font stack today, so nothing feels unfamiliar.

05

Buttons & CTAs

Rounded, confident, few. The red call button carries the phone number wherever it goes; navy handles everything routine.

The 24/7 chip rides next to CTAs as quiet reassurance — it is never a button itself.
