Air Comfort Inc. design system

Warm and cool in balance. An HVAC company sells comfort, so the site should feel comfortable: a warm off-white base, terracotta for heating, slate blue for cooling, rounded corners, and room to breathe. Modern and straightforward. The less clutter the better.

Colours

The palette is the concept: terracotta stands for heating, slate blue for cooling, and both sit quietly on a warm off-white. Blue is the primary action colour. Terracotta is the accent and never appears on more than one button per screen. Tints do the background work so the full colours stay special.

Sora for headings: geometric, friendly and modern without trying too hard. Inter for everything else. Headings are sentence case, short, and say what the section is. One colour accent word per headline at most, always in blue.

Buttons and links

Pills only. Blue is the default action. The terracotta button is reserved for the single most important call on the page, usually "Call now". White buttons live on dark panels.

Forms

Rounded fields, real labels, soft blue focus ring. The estimate form asks only for what the office actually needs. No asterisk forests.

Photography

Real trucks, real techs, real Idaho homes. Rounded corners on every photo. Avoid stock photos of strangers pointing at thermostats. The placeholder pattern below is used across all mockups: add a src to the img and the label disappears.

Voice and usage

The site should read like the office answers the phone: helpful, plain, confident. Forty years in the valley is the proof, so state things simply and let the reviews do the bragging.

Always

  • Sentence case headings that say what the section is
  • Phone number visible without scrolling
  • One clear action per section
  • Warm tint for heating, cool tint for cooling
  • Real reviews with real names
  • Rounded corners: 18px, 28px for large panels

Never

  • Em dashes, kickers or eyebrow labels
  • Nine-item dropdown menus
  • Gradient text, glows or glassmorphism
  • "Elevate", "seamless", "unlock", "solutions"
  • Two competing buttons side by side
  • Harsh reds or icy neon blues
