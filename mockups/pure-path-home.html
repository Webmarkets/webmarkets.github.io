How the program works.

Three natural processes do the heavy lifting. Your coach makes sure you get through them safely and comfortably.

1

Your body enters ketosis

After roughly 48 hours, your body switches fuel sources and begins burning stored fat for energy.

2

Harmful fat goes first

Ketosis prioritises visceral fat, the harmful fat that surrounds your organs, which is why the process supports overall health.

3

Natural cellular cleanup

Alongside ketosis, autophagy breaks down and removes old and damaged cells. A natural detox, no products required.
